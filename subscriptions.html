Good to know

When am I charged?

Your subscription is billed weekly, before each delivery. You will see the weekly total before you confirm.

Can I change my meals?

Yes. Swap any meals from your account before the order cut-off for that delivery.

Can I pause or cancel?

Any time, with no fees. Pause for a week or two, or cancel from your account. No contracts.

Where do you deliver?

UK-wide by DPD every Wednesday and Sunday, in insulated bags with gel packs. You can also collect from our Edinburgh store.
